How it works

From a dial in a cattle camp to a decision in minutes.

Built for places with long distances, few vets and patchy signal.

Report

Dial a short code on any phone, or use the field app, which saves reports offline and sends them when signal returns.

Triage

Signs are matched against priority diseases. Anthrax, Rift Valley Fever and rabies raise an urgent alert and safety advice at once.

Warn

Clusters and sudden spikes are detected across county lines, vets are texted, and herders can check risk before moving.

Always watching for

The diseases that matter most in pastoral herds.

Built for the field

Everything a surveillance system needs, nothing it can't use.

Offline first

The field app stores reports on the phone and syncs safely later. Resending never creates duplicates.

Any phone

USSD menus run on every handset and network, with no data plan and no app to install.

Movement advisories

Before migrating, herders and health workers see whether a destination is clear, needs caution, or should be avoided.

Early warning

Uses the CDC's EARS-C2 method to catch sudden rises in a county, plus distance-based clusters across borders.

Privacy by design

Researchers get anonymised data only: no names, no GPS, and small groups hidden so no herd can be singled out.

Vets decide

Triage ranks what to rule out first; it never diagnoses or prescribes. Every clinical action is on the record.
